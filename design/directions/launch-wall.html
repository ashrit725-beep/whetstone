Four moves, start to shipped.

01Post the build
A link, a cut, a draft or a repo, plus the stage it is at on the grit scale. The stage and the kind of project decide which questions the composer puts in front of a reviewer.
02Name the ask
You write the questions. A reviewer reads your ask before they open the build, which is what keeps the reply about the part you are unsure of rather than the part that is easiest to comment on.
03Read the disagreement
Notes are grouped by theme, and every rating is drawn as a spread rather than an average. Where reviewers disagree is the finding, so Whetstone shows you the split and names the outliers instead of flattening five people into one number.
04Answer or ship it
Turn a note into a suggestion and move it from open to planned to shipped. The reviewer who raised it is told when it moves. Six suggestions have been marked shipped so far.

Every note carries three things.

Which is why 233 points sort into a work list instead of a wall of opinion.

Salon Hang, measured.

Every ratio below is computed, not estimated. Citron is the identity mark and it is a fill only: it always carries near-black ink on it and a perceivable border, because citron against light paper is 1.24:1 and would fail as a line or a word. In light the text role of that family is olive #4C5A0E.

Rules that hold in both themes

No state is carried by hue alone. Severity ships a word, a border and a filled square. The median ships a citron bar and the word “median”.
One citron object per viewport at most, plus the grit marker. Measured accent footprint on both screens is under 2% of pixels.
The hairline never bounds a control. Any edge you must see to operate something is --edge, 3.6:1 or better everywhere.
The artwork is literal colour, not tokens: it is the creator’s media and it renders identically in both themes.

Border and shadow
1px in every state. Width never changes on hover or focus, only colour. Focus is a 2px outline at 3px offset, never a border swap.
No shadow on any card, tile, row, chip or button anywhere in this file. Depth is the 1px rule, the surface step, and negative space. One overlay shadow token exists for modals: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(10,13,9,.55).
Maximum one containment level. Nothing bordered sits inside anything bordered.
