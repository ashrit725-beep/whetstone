Signal Room

Ink on warm paper, one saturated flare, and a stage that is always night. Whetstone is a room where signals come back from people who looked at your work, so the product is built out of type, rule and drawn composition rather than cards, and the one accent is spent only where a person is waiting for an answer.

How it
works.

Four moves. The fourth is the one nobody else does.

01
Post the build
A link, a cut, a draft or a build, plus the grit step it is standing on. The step decides which questions the composer puts in front of a reviewer.
02
Name the asks
You write the questions. A reviewer reads them before they open the work, so the note comes back about the part you are unsure of, not the part that is easiest to comment on.
03
Read the disagreement
Every note carries a category, a severity and the version it was written against. Where reviewers split, Whetstone draws the split. It never averages people into a number nobody said.
04
Ship it, and say so
Turn a note into a change, move it to shipped, and the reviewer who raised it is told. Six people have had that message so far.

Every note lands in one of five categories.

Spacing, radius, borders, shadow, motion

ScaleValues
space4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96 · 144px. Section rhythm alternates 96 / 144 / 96 / 64 so no two neighbours breathe the same. Internal padding of a container never exceeds its external spacing.
radius0px panels, rails, stage slabs, table cells · 2px chips, stamps, tokens, thumbnails · 4px buttons and inputs · 8px reserved for popovers · 14px reserved for the composer dialog · 9999px on avatars only. No pill buttons anywhere.
border1px in every state. Width never changes on hover or focus, only colour: --edge to --ink-1. Error is the one exception and steps to 2px --flare, always with a text message. Maximum one containment level: no bordered box inside a bordered box.
shadowNone on panels, rows, notes or the stage. One token exists, 0 18px 40px -18px rgba(20,18,13,.45), and it is spent only on the composer dialog and popovers. Elevation is carried by rules and ground steps.
motion120ms colour and hover · 180ms control state · 240ms entrance · 140ms exit, always shorter than its entrance. Entrance cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1), exit cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1). Nothing over 240ms, no scroll triggered reveals, no parallax, no counters. Every duration collapses to 1ms under prefers-reduced-motion.
targets48px primary controls, 36px compact controls, 8px minimum gap between adjacent targets. SC 2.5.8 passes with margin.

Rules that hold the thing together

One accentThe system is ink, paper and flare. No second hue exists, in either theme. Severity, status and sentiment are drawn with stepped marks plus a written word, so every state survives greyscale. Flare covers under 2% of either screen.
The stage is always night#17140E in both themes. Project media never inverts, so a project looks the same to everyone who links to it.
Nothing is a grey boxWhere there is no photograph there is a drawn composition: posterised bands, hairline trunks, an outline word sliced across the offset slabs, and a timecode ruler whose flare tick sits at the exact second the creator asked about.
